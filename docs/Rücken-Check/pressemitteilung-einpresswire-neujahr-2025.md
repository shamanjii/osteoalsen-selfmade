# Hamburg Osteopath Launches Free Back Pain Assessment Program for New Year 2025

**VFO-Certified Practitioner Joshua Alsen Offers Limited Complimentary Consultations Plus Evidence-Based Exercise Plans to Help Hamburg Residents Start 2025 Pain-Free**

**Hamburg, Germany – January 1, 2025** – As millions of people worldwide set New Year's resolutions focused on health and wellness, Hamburg-based osteopath Joshua Alsen is launching a unique initiative to help residents tackle one of the most common health complaints: chronic back pain. Starting January 6, 2025, Osteopathie Alsen will offer 20 complimentary 10-minute back pain assessments, complete with personalized exercise plans designed to provide lasting relief.

## Addressing Hamburg's Back Pain Epidemic

Back pain affects approximately 80% of adults at some point in their lives, with many cases becoming chronic due to modern lifestyle factors such as prolonged sitting, poor posture, and stress. In Hamburg, as in many urban centers, the combination of desk-based work and reduced physical activity has created a growing need for accessible, evidence-based treatment options.

"Every January, I see patients who've been living with back pain for months or even years, finally deciding to take action as part of their New Year's health goals," explains Joshua Alsen, B.Sc. Osteopathy, a VFO-certified osteopath practicing in Hamburg's Rotherbaum and Eimsbüttel neighborhoods. "This free assessment program is designed to remove barriers and give people the guidance they need to start their journey toward a pain-free life."

## Comprehensive 10-Minute Assessment Process

The complimentary back pain check includes:

**Diagnostic Evaluation:** A structured questionnaire covering pain location, duration, intensity, and triggering factors to identify the specific type of back pain (mechanical, radicular, muscular-fascial, cervical, or chronic/complex).

**Red Flag Screening:** Professional assessment to rule out serious underlying conditions requiring immediate medical attention.

**Personalized Recommendation:** Clear guidance on whether osteopathic treatment is appropriate, how many sessions might be needed, and what complementary therapies could help.

**Evidence-Based Exercise Plan:** Each participant receives one of five specialized training programs (delivered via email) tailored to their specific pain type:
- Type A: Mechanical lower back/sacroiliac pain (4-week program)
- Type B: Radicular pain/sciatica (6-week program)
- Type C: Muscular-fascial pain (4-week program with foam rolling)
- Type D: Cervical spine/neck pain (4-week program)
- Type E: Chronic/complex pain (8-week multimodal program)

## Evidence-Based Osteopathic Care

Joshua Alsen completed a rigorous 4-year professional training program earning a Bachelor of Science in Osteopathy and maintains certification with the Verband Freier Osteopathen (VFO), Germany's leading professional osteopathic organization. His approach combines three pillars of osteopathic medicine:

**Parietal Osteopathy:** Treatment of the musculoskeletal system, including joints, muscles, and fascia
**Visceral Osteopathy:** Addressing organ mobility and its impact on the musculoskeletal system
**Craniosacral Osteopathy:** Gentle techniques for the skull, spine, and nervous system

"Osteopathy views the body as an integrated whole," Alsen notes. "Often, back pain isn't just about the back—it can be related to digestive issues, stress, poor ergonomics, or movement patterns developed over years. Our comprehensive approach addresses root causes, not just symptoms."

## Two Convenient Hamburg Locations

Osteopathie Alsen operates from two locations in Hamburg to serve patients across the city:

## Insurance Coverage: 40-60€ Reimbursement Available

One common barrier to osteopathic care is cost awareness. Many Hamburg residents don't realize that German statutory health insurance (gesetzliche Krankenkasse) typically reimburses 40-60 euros per osteopathy session. Private insurance plans usually cover the full treatment cost of 150 euros per session.

"We provide detailed invoices that make the reimbursement process straightforward," Alsen explains. "Many patients are pleasantly surprised to learn their health insurance will subsidize their treatment."

## Digital Health Resources

In addition to the free assessment program, participants gain access to Osteopathie Alsen's comprehensive online resources:

**Blog Archive:** Over 23 evidence-based articles (66,000+ words) covering topics from sciatica treatment to sports osteopathy, all grounded in current scientific research

**FAQ Section:** 32 frequently asked questions addressing costs, treatment processes, insurance coverage, and condition-specific information

**Video Guides:** Demonstration videos for all exercise programs, ensuring proper form and technique

## How to Book Your Free Assessment

The complimentary back pain assessment program runs throughout January 2025, with only 20 slots available on a first-come, first-served basis. Interested Hamburg residents can book online at www.osteoalsen.de/terminbuchung or call 0176 43990001.

Appointments are conducted via telephone, making them accessible for those with mobility limitations or busy schedules. Each assessment takes approximately 10 minutes, with personalized exercise plans and recommendations delivered via email within 24 hours.

## Commitment to Health Education

The launch of this program reflects Osteopathie Alsen's broader commitment to patient education and empowerment. "Our goal isn't just to treat pain—it's to give people the knowledge and tools to manage their own health," Alsen states. "These exercise programs are designed to be done at home, with no special equipment, in just 10-15 minutes per day. Real change comes from consistent daily habits, not just occasional treatments."

The evidence-based exercise plans incorporate current best practices from osteopathic medicine, physiotherapy, and sports science, with progressive programming that adapts as patients improve over 4-8 weeks.

## About Osteopathie Alsen

Founded by Joshua Alsen, B.Sc. Osteopathy, Osteopathie Alsen is a holistic manual medicine practice serving Hamburg and surrounding areas. The practice specializes in treating acute and chronic conditions of the musculoskeletal system, digestive system, and nervous system, with particular focus on back pain, headaches, sports injuries, and digestive disorders.

All treatments follow the principles of parietal, visceral, and craniosacral osteopathy, using exclusively manual techniques without medication or invasive procedures. The practice serves patients of all ages, from infants to seniors.
